(01)Why paper still matters

A poster has three seconds to stop a stranger. No algorithm, no feed, no second chance — just paper, a wall and an idea loud enough to be seen. Blauwerk is a year-long celebration of the people who make those three seconds count.

Fourth edition, since 2019

Run by a non-profit foundation in Rotterdam

Every poster on the wall was printed at least once in the real world
